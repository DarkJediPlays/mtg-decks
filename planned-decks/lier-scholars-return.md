# The Scholar's Return — Lier, Disciple of the Drowned

**Commander:** Lier, Disciple of the Drowned
**Colours:** Izzet (UR)
**Archetype:** Spellslinger / Graveyard Recursion / Double-Cast Engine
**Bracket:** 3-4
**Estimated build cost:** ~£80-120

---

## How It Wins

Lier has two abilities: (1) spells can't be countered, (2) every instant and sorcery
in your graveyard gains Flashback equal to its mana cost.

The plan: fill your graveyard with the best instants and sorceries in the game,
then cast them all again. Storm-Kiln Artist generates a Treasure for every cast and
copy, funding the repeated flashback activations. Every spell you've ever cast becomes
available again the moment Lier hits the table.

Key note: while Lier is in play, NO ONE can counter spells — including you.
This deck therefore runs zero counterspells. Your protection is Lightning Greaves
and the speed of the engine.

**Win conditions:**
- Repeated Lightning Bolts + burn spell recursion
- Niv-Mizzet, Parun pinging for massive damage off every spell and draw
- Guttersnipe + Firebrand Archer dealing 3-4 per spell across a long storm turn
- Mizzix's Mastery overloaded — cast every instant and sorcery in your GY simultaneously
- Underworld Breach recurring the graveyard from exile and winning through accumulated value
