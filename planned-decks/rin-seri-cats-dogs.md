# It's Raining Cats & Dogs — Rin and Seri, Inseparable

**Commander:** Rin and Seri, Inseparable
**Colours:** Naya (RGW)
**Archetype:** Cat/Dog Tribal Token Flood / Proliferate / Counter Synergy
**Bracket:** 3
**Deck name:** It's Raining Cats & Dogs

---

## How It Wins

Every Cat spell you cast creates a Dog token. Every Dog spell creates a Cat token.
With Anointed Procession: every Cat creates 2 Dog tokens, every Dog creates 2 Cat tokens.
Cast 10 Cat/Dog creatures and you have a 30-creature board.

Rin and Seri's activated ability: {3}{R}{G}{W} — for each Cat you control, target creature gets
+1/+0. For each Dog, target creature gains trample. With 20 Cats on board, Rin turns
your entire army into 20+/X trampling attackers in a single activation.

Proliferate adds +1/+1 counters to every token simultaneously — Ajani's Pridemate grows
on every life gain, Hardened Scales adds an extra counter to every placement, Branching
Evolution doubles every +1/+1 counter placed.

Path of Ancestry scries every time you cast a Cat or Dog — which is every creature spell.

**The speed goal:** Rin on turn 3 via Sol Ring/Arcane Signet. Three cheap Cats/Dogs
in turns 1-2 give you six total creatures before Rin lands. Turn 4 you're playing
into a board of 10+ with Anointed Procession on the table.
