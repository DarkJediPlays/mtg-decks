**Bracket:** 3-4

---

## How It Wins

Cast all five Paradigm cards once (Jodah lets you pay WUBRG instead of their
actual mana costs — 5 mana for all of them). They exile themselves. From that
point, at the beginning of each of your main phases, you may cast free copies
of each:

- **Restoration Seminar** — reanimate any permanent from your GY every turn
- **Echocasting Symposium** — clone your best creature every turn
- **Decorum Dissertation** — draw 2 / drain 2 from any player every turn
- **Improvisation Capstone** — cascade into free high-CMC spells every turn
- **Germination Practicum** — put two +1/+1 counters on EVERY creature you control every turn

With Doubling Season: Germination Practicum puts four counters per creature per turn.
With Corpsejack Menace AND Doubling Season: eight counters per creature per turn.

Fathom Mage draws a card each time she gets a counter — she draws once per turn
minimum from Germination Practicum alone. Lorescale Coatl grows from
Decorum Dissertation's draws. Iridescent Hornbeetle creates an Insect token for
every counter placed each turn — potentially dozens per turn cycle.
Kalonian Hydra doubles ALL counters on ALL creatures when attacking.
Champion of Lambholt grows from every creature entering and makes your
entire army unblockable once her power exceeds opponent creature power.

Zaffai and the Tempests (owned x3) is in the 99 — he also gives you one
additional free instant or sorcery cast per turn on top of all five Paradigm effects.
