## How It Wins

Chain instants and sorceries to generate explosive turns filled with copied spells and
massive damage bursts. Esior protects the commanders while Ludevic keeps cards flowing.
Payoffs like Thousand-Year Storm, Guttersnipe, Ral Storm Conduit, and Niv-Mizzet turn
every spell into a devastating threat.

**Key combos:**
- Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame — infinite hasty creature copies
- Niv-Mizzet, Parun + Curiosity — infinite draw/damage loop
- Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind + Curiosity — same
- Dualcaster Mage + Rite of Replication — infinite ETBs
- Dualcaster Mage + Irenicus's Vile Duplication — infinite copies
- Niv-Mizzet, Parun + Ophidian Eye — infinite draw/damage
- Mana Geyser + Reiterate — infinite mana
- Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind + Ophidian Eye — infinite draw/damage
- Walk the Aeons (extra turn)
