# Trance State — Kuja, Genome Sorcerer

**Commander:** Kuja, Genome Sorcerer // Trance Kuja, Fate Defied
**Colours:** Rakdos (BR)
**Archetype:** Spellburn / Wizard Tribal / Token Ping Storm
**Power Level:** 7 | **Bracket:** 2
**Source:** Collectors Cove — £139.99

---

## How It Wins

Kuja creates a 0/1 black Wizard token at each end step. Once you control
four or more Wizards (including Kuja), he transforms into Trance Kuja,
Fate Defied — a 4/6 that makes all your Wizards deal double their damage.

Every cheap spell you cast triggers Firebrand Archer, Electrostatic Field,
Electrostatic Infantry, Kessig Flamebreather, Guttersnipe and Coruscation Mage
simultaneously. With Trance Kuja's doubling active, each ping becomes 2 damage,
each Guttersnipe hit becomes 4 per opponent.

**2-Card Combo:** Dualcaster Mage + Saw in Half — copy Saw in Half targeting
Dualcaster Mage, creating infinite Dualcaster Mage copies entering the battlefield,
each pinging via impact tremors-style effects.
