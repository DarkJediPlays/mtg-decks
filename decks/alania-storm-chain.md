# Tempest Artist — Alania, Divergent Storm

**Commander:** Alania, Divergent Storm
**Colours:** Izzet (UR)
**Archetype:** Storm / Spell Copy Chain / Go Wide Token Flood
**Bracket:** 3-4
**Source:** Combined from I Say Thee ZAP! + Prismari Artistry precon + Kuja (UR-legal cards)

---

## How It Wins

Alania copies your first instant, first sorcery, and first Otter spell each turn —
three potential free copies per turn. Combined with Thousand-Year Storm, Twinning Staff,
Howl of the Horde, and Reiterate, a single turn spirals into a cascade of copies.

Each copy triggers your wide-board engines:
- Young Pyromancer / Murmuring Mystic / Deekah — 1/1 token per spell/copy
- Manaform Hellkite — X/X flying Dragon per instant or sorcery (X = CMC)
- Saheeli, Sublime Artificer — 1/1 Servo per noncreature spell
- Rionya, Fire Dancer — at combat, clone your best creature once per spell cast

Finish with the entire token army swinging, Magma Opus refuelling, or
Aminatou's Augury cascading into free multiple spells in one activation.

Stormcatch Mentor and Goblin Electromancer keep the chain going cheaply.
Coruscation Mage pings each opponent on every noncreature spell.

**Kuja** — stays as a separate Bracket 2 casual BR deck.
Coruscation Mage goes here from Kuja since it's Bloomburrow red.
