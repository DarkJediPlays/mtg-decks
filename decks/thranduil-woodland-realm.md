## ⚠️ BRACKET 4 FLAGS — DISCLOSE BEFORE EVERY GAME

**Demonic Consultation + Thassa's Oracle** — instant win combo: name a card

## How It Wins

**Primary — The Ability Engine:**
Get Devoted Druid, Wirewood Channeler, and Wirewood Symbiote into the graveyard.
Thranduil now has all three activated abilities simultaneously:

- Tap (using Channeler's ability): add {G} per Elf you control
- Return an Elf from hand, untap a creature (Wirewood Symbiote): untap Thranduil
- Net result: tap Thranduil for 10+ green mana, return one Elf to hand, untap Thranduil, repeat — infinite green mana

**Buried Alive** is the one-card setup: put Devoted Druid + Wirewood Channeler +
Wirewood Symbiote into your graveyard. Thranduil activates all three next turn.

With infinite mana: Exsanguinate for lethal X, or Staff of Domination draws
your entire library, then Walking Ballista for infinite X damage to each opponent.

**Secondary — Thassa's Oracle:**
Cast Demonic Consultation, name a card not in your deck, exile entire library.
Cast Thassa's Oracle — wins the game immediately. Can be done at instant speed
on opponents' turns with Fierce Guardianship backup.

**Tertiary — Legendary Elf Chain:**
Every legendary Elf entering draws 2 cards from Thranduil's second ability.
Lathril, Thranduil Sindarin Liege, Marwyn, Elvish Harbinger, Selvala, and Elrond
all trigger this. Drawing 10+ cards in a single turn chain is not unusual.

**Backup Thranduil — Necrotic Ooze:**
Necrotic Ooze has ALL activated abilities from ALL creature cards in ALL graveyards.
When Thranduil is removed, the Ooze immediately takes over the full combo.

## COMBO REFERENCE CARD

**The Core Loop (get these into GY via Buried Alive):**

| Elf in GY | Ability Thranduil Gains |
|---|---|
| Wirewood Channeler | Tap for {G} per Elf you control (main mana source) |
| Devoted Druid | Put -1/-1 counter: untap self (on a 5/6 Thranduil: doesn't kill him) |
| Wirewood Symbiote | Return an Elf to hand: untap target creature |

**The Loop:**
1. Tap Thranduil (Channeler ability) — add 8G with 8 Elves
2. Return an Elf from hand, untap Thranduil (Symbiote ability)
3. Tap Thranduil again — add 7G (one Elf fewer in hand)
4. Use Devoted Druid's -1/-1 counter ability instead for free untap
5. With Training Grounds reducing all costs, loop becomes trivially profitable
6. Infinite green mana → Exsanguinate / Walking Ballista / Staff of Domination

**Buried Alive Setup:** Put Wirewood Channeler + Wirewood Symbiote + Devoted Druid into GY in one sorcery cast. Thranduil attacks next turn and you have infinite mana.

**Gifts Ungiven Setup:** Search for Devoted Druid + Wirewood Channeler + Wirewood Symbiote + Priest of Titania. Opponent puts 2 in your hand, 2 in GY. You need them in the GY — this always advances the plan.

**Hermit Druid Setup (aggressive):** With minimal basic lands in the deck, one activation dumps your entire creature suite into the GY, giving Thranduil every elf ability simultaneously.

**Instant Win:** Cast Demonic Consultation, name "Mountain". Every card in your library is exiled. Cast Thassa's Oracle — you win. Total mana cost: {B} + {U}{U} = 3 mana on any turn.
